This summer's travel is to Maritime 3 provinces: New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Nova Scotia. We were planning to go ourselves, but it's such a long route like 3 thousands km that we finally gave up and went with the travel group, thinking of having a real relax vacation, and it's always the favorite way of Julie.

<a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEg8j02W_wj3WKFwEgAZefE4xhazEXiyOlAifCK_iiB3_vrnhOGz9Tms8PCS1pIzwr0KszP4urW4vrV6nMj_rRRSp9bzvg0m8yE9X4jLHeTaf6hNnoEdSjfbIRzHGOZSVbANc4de6-R_llc/s1600/IMG_1158.JPG" imageanchor="1" style="clear: right; float: right; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-left: 1em;"><img border="0" height="150" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEg8j02W_wj3WKFwEgAZefE4xhazEXiyOlAifCK_iiB3_vrnhOGz9Tms8PCS1pIzwr0KszP4urW4vrV6nMj_rRRSp9bzvg0m8yE9X4jLHeTaf6hNnoEdSjfbIRzHGOZSVbANc4de6-R_llc/s200/IMG_1158.JPG" width="200" /></a>Pictures and videos can describe all those wonderful attractions we enjoyed. What impressed us are the maritime climate with blue sky, green plain, proper city and nice people. Comparing to Montreal, the big and noisy city with always humid weather, we began to dream of living somewhere in Charlottown or Halifax...

We got back to reality when we came back home: work, school, cooking, cleaning, washing, etc. That's life, with not only the beautiful sites, but also troubles, challenges, hard works, successes or failures, lucky or unlucky things... which is beautiful too!
