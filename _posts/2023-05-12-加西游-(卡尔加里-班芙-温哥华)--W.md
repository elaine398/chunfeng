In August 2022, we had a wonderful travel in Canada west, especially in Banff and Jasper national park. We were so shocked by the beauty of Canadian Rockies with so many splendid scenes!
We also visited some lovely towns and cities between Jasper and Vancouver.
And finally we stopped driving in Vancouver. Together with my brother's family, we had a super happy time by visiting the downtown, parks, beaches, museums, university, etc. and of course, the delicious foods of Vancouver!
So here is my vlog for those wonderful days!
